### Foundry IQ with D365F&O and Fabric IQ

- **Link**: [https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/azure-architecture-blog/foundry-iq-with-d365f-o-and-fabric-iq/ba-p/4557381](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/t5/azure-architecture-blog/foundry-iq-with-d365f-o-and-fabric-iq/ba-p/4557381)

- **Fetched**: 2026-09-18 05:38:27

<details class="entry-summary">
<summary>詳細を表示</summary>
<p><strong>Business Scenario</strong></p>
<p>A retail organization runs <strong>multiple promotional sales events</strong> across its store network in different cities, featuring various product categories where Data is captured from third-party systems (products, stores, sales events) and D365 Finance and Operations (customer data)</p>
<p>Customers are linked to stores <strong>based on city</strong>, and each store hosts specific sales events.</p>
<p>To figure out the <strong>store generating highest revenue</strong>, store event driving the <strong>best outcome</strong>, majority <strong>customer footprint</strong>, the organization has implemented copilot studio agent using Fabric IQ, Ontology and ERP MCP.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong><em>New Business Challenge</em></strong></p>
<p>Beyond understanding past performance, the retailer wants the system to answer:</p>
<ul>
<li>Are there procurement risks impacting promotional sales?</li>
<li>Which cities should receive additional marketing spend?</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong><em>Solution</em></strong></p>
<p>Create a foundry agent using Foundry IQ including Fabric IQ, Web IQ, Web IQ as knowledge source.</p>
<p><strong>Foundry IQ is not the source of data</strong>. Its primary role is the <strong>reasoning, orchestration, and decision-making layer</strong> that connects insights from Fabric IQ, Work IQ, and Web IQ , sharepoint, blob, custom agents etc and generates business recommendations. It is backed by Azure AI search to get an indexed search with accurate result.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>A. Prerequisite:</strong></p>
<p><strong>Data Sources &amp; Relationships Overview</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Product, store, and sales event data are sourced from <strong>third-party systems </strong>.</li>
<li>Customer data is sourced from the <strong>ERP (Dynamics 365 Finance &amp; Operations)</strong> system through Fabric.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>How the data is connected:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Customers are linked to stores based on <strong>city alignment</strong> (customer city = store location).</li>
<li>Sales events are associated with:</li>
</ul>
<p>o&nbsp;&nbsp; The <strong>products being sold</strong>, and</p>
<p>o&nbsp;&nbsp; The <strong>stores where the events are conducted</strong>.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<ul>
<li>Country specific marketing policy documents stored in sharepoint</li>
</ul>

<p>is exactly the type of cross-domain reasoning scenario where the agent combines:</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fabric IQ Ontology</strong> → understands relationships between Store, Product, SaleEvent, Customer.</li>
<li><strong>F&amp;O ERP MCP</strong> → retrieves operational procurement data such as PO status, Confirmed Delivery Date, Vendor performance, Inventory levels.</li>
<li><strong>SharePoint Policy Repository</strong> → validates country-specific marketing investment rules and campaign approval constraints.</li>
<li><strong>Foundry IQ/Web Intelligence (if enabled)</strong> → checks supplier news, disruptions, external market events.</li>
</ul>
